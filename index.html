📏 Reference Ranges
Blood Pressure
Normal: 90–120 / 60–80 mmHg
High: >130/80 · Low: <90/60
Blood Sugar
Normal: 3.9–5.5 mmol/L (fasting)
Pre-DM: 5.6–6.9 · DM: ≥7.0
Heart Rate
Normal: 60–100 bpm
Brady: <60 · Tachy: >100
SpO₂
Normal: ≥95%
Critical: <90%
Temperature
Normal: 97–99°F
Fever: >100.4°F · Hypo: <96°F
